## Source hierarchy

1. Printed page images of 1871, 1875, 1903, and 1920 editions.
2. OCR tied to a specific scan and page.
3. Project Gutenberg HTML/plain text for discovery and digital-object analysis.
4. Derived alignments, counts, and graphs.

The Project Gutenberg object is also a research target in its own right: it presents a “complete Yule-Cordier edition” assembled from the 1903 volumes and 1920 additions. Its organization therefore cannot substitute for the historical printed witnesses.
