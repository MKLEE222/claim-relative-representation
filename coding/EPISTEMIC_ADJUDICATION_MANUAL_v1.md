# Epistemic Adjudication Manual v1

Date frozen: 2026-09-25
Status: STAGE B - HUMAN ADJUDICATION ONLY

## Input

Stage B receives:

1. a frozen humanistic claim/question;
2. source passages admitted by the task/access contract;
3. verified or recovered Stage-A documentary state;
4. the warrant policy below.

It does not receive:
- author provisional decisions;
- expected mechanism name;
- transformation condition name.

## Relation labels

- E_SUPPORT
- E_UNDERCUT
- E_QUALIFY_AUTHORITY
- E_REHABILITATE
- E_ACCRETION
- E_IDENTIFICATION_UPDATE
- E_UNRESOLVED

## Warrant states

### RETAIN
The claim remains usable as stated under the supplied evidence and declared task.

### REVISE
The supplied evidence supports a materially narrower/corrected formulation rather than the claim as stated.

### DEFER
Materially different epistemic assignments remain live and the contract does not license choosing among them.

### WITHHOLD
The supplied evidence does not license using the claim as stated for the declared scholarly task.

## Separation rule

Relation adjudication and warrant-state adjudication are recorded separately.

A relation label does not mechanically dictate a warrant state.

For example:
- an undercut may be immaterial to the exact claim;
- a rehabilitation may restore only part of a claim;
- an accretion may justify WITHHOLD for one interpretive task while leaving a descriptive base claim RETAIN.

## Evidence discipline

Use only supplied source/documentary state.

Do not consult:
- external scholarship unless the condition explicitly permits H2;
- author ledger;
- previous condition responses.

## Confidence / unresolved policy

If two warrant states remain materially defensible under the supplied evidence:
choose DEFER and state the unresolved dependency.

Do not guess for forced agreement.
