## Epistemic relation definitions

- E_SUPPORT: supplied evidence increases entitlement to the frozen claim.
- E_UNDERCUT: supplied evidence weakens entitlement to the frozen claim or a premise/source authority on which it depends.
- E_QUALIFY_AUTHORITY: supplied evidence narrows authority, eyewitness status, scope, or conditions without necessarily negating descriptive content.
- E_REHABILITATE: supplied evidence defeats or substantially weakens an earlier undercutting reason and thereby restores entitlement previously reduced.
- E_ACCRETION: an editorial layer adds an interpretation at a broader explanatory/historical scale than the base observation alone licenses.
- E_IDENTIFICATION_UPDATE: supplied evidence changes entitlement to a prior identification of place/person/source/referent/material.
- E_UNRESOLVED: the available state permits materially different epistemic assignments under this contract.

## Admissible warrant states

### RETAIN
The atomic mulberry-material claim remains admissible as evidence under the supplied editorial record.

### DEFER
Materially different assignments remain live and the packet does not license a stable decision on the atomic claim.

### WITHHOLD
The supplied record does not license using the atomic mulberry-material claim as evidence.

`REVISE` is not an admissible output for this final atomic task.

## No broader-claim escape

Evidence for the existence, scale, denominations, circulation, or administration of paper-money does not by itself answer the atomic material-identification claim.

Such evidence may be context, but the adjudicator must not return RETAIN merely because a broader paper-money proposition is well supported.
