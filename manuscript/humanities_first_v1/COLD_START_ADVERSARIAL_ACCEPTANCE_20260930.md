## Acceptance standard

A reader unfamiliar with the project should be able to identify, from the article itself: (1) the scholarly question and DH contribution; (2) the printed sources and the actors and relations in each close reading; (3) what the controlled comparison changes and holds fixed; (4) what each denominator counts; (5) why Formalization Papers appears as a bounded mechanism comparison; and (6) which findings are interpretive, corrected reproduction, diagnostic, or unresolved. The PDF must compile, render its two figures and three tables legibly, and contain no working-draft headers or internal evidence tags in the main argument.

## Checklist items that do not map to this study

The paper neither trains a predictive model nor estimates population-level performance. Train/test leakage, hyperparameters, random seeds, cross-validation, variance across runs, model-family baselines, error bars, p-values, confidence intervals, and multiple-comparison correction are therefore inapplicable to the claims presented. The relevant numerical audit is denominator provenance, exhaustive enumeration under a fixed grammar, negative controls, and clear separation of documentary items from constructed counterfactuals. No effect size or statistical significance is claimed.

## Open submission gates and non-claims

1. Compare the short quotations and surrounding paragraphs with the fixed 1903 and 1920 page images, recording printed-page versus scan-index correspondence. The current revision checked accessible digitized text and earlier page-verification records, not a fresh fixed-image adjudication.
2. The blinded three-model historical audit produced no stable consensus on the 21 declared components. Independent human historical validation has not been obtained. The article reports this partial result without treating it as source confirmation.
3. Complete a targeted novelty/priority check against nearby DH editorial modelling, provenance, versioning, and scholarship-as-activity work. The article currently positions itself against selected, checked neighbours rather than an exhaustive literature field.
4. Verify reuse terms for the frozen Formalization Papers snapshot. The public repository README identifies the supplementary study and Zenodo link, but a repository LICENSE file was not found in this check.
5. Before submission, add author-approved declarations for AI assistance, conflicts of interest, and any journal-specific data/code availability wording. No human annotator agreement or sensitive-data claim should be invented.

## Scientific-discipline notes

The Paper Money and Arbre Sec sequences were exposed analytic cases, not a random or prospective historical sample. The loss in Figure 2 is a constructed representation ablation; native plain-text recovery was observed in an earlier audit when relevant passages were available. The first Formalization prospective execution was invalid; the successful run is a corrected reproduction using the identical archive, and later comparisons are diagnostic. The 52 chains share roots and records. The finite rule-conformance checks test the consequences of declared interpretations; they cannot independently establish those interpretations as historical facts.
