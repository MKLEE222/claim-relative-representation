## 0. Authority and scope

The user has explicitly rejected shrinking the mother problem until the existing experiments suffice for a paper. This audit reopens research design and evidentiary obligations, not frozen experimental outcomes. It supersedes the planning recommendation to stop research and write the bounded paper. It does not turn any old failure into a pass.

The upper research question is the user's computational-representation problem: what representations suffice for reliable and sustained warranted discovery? The repository's narrower scholarly version asks what source-grounded relations and lawful research routes must survive representational change for editorial knowledge history to remain researchable. The proposed experiments are tests of parts of that question, not definitions that replace the whole question.

The 2026-09-26 RESEARCH_PLAN already explicitly required strengthening the contribution rather than reducing claims until publishable. It also required native baselines, matched information access, substantive historical consequences, and separation of decoder recovery, projection separation, and interpretive consequence. Those requirements remain active.

No claim is made here that a three-level hierarchy, seven-dimensional profile, or new notation is itself a theoretical contribution. A long sequence of known queries is not, merely by being long, a test of discovery.

## 1. What the mother problem requires us to distinguish

| Dimension | Operational obligation | What the old evidence does not establish |
|---|---|---|
| Reliable | Robustness across source-supported alternatives, representations, policies and perturbations; correct uncertainty and abstention | Repeatable outputs or green CI are not historical correctness |
| Sustained | Retained state remains useful after new evidence, question changes, revision and repeated handoffs | One fixed-target path is not a continuing inquiry |
| Warranted | A conclusion has a checkable source basis, attribution, scope and status under an explicit inference/admissibility contract | Exact citation text or model agreement is not entailment or historical truth |
| Discovery | Previously unavailable-to-the-researcher questions/distinctions become identifiable and investigable from the current evidence | Finding a supplied target measures access, not question emergence |
| Representation sufficiency | A specified interface retains or lawfully recovers distinctions needed for a declared continuation family | A bag of required output fields or one successful decoder is not a universal sufficient representation |

Discovery in the next bounded experiments means new-to-the-declared-research-state, not new-to-human-scholarship. The latter requires separate historical evaluation. Sustained means continuation over declared events and horizons; it does not mean arbitrary future discovery has been solved.

## 3. Concrete implementation finding: overlap is not completeness

The current r3_aw1_great_desert_component_audit_v1.py calls the inherited overlap predicate both for component_rank and collective_complete. That predicate is:

    w.char_hi > lo and w.char_lo < hi

This requires any intersection, not containment. A one-character intersection can pass. The labels single_window_complete and collective_complete therefore overstate what their predicates certify.

Disposition:

- Keep every old rank and raw output unchanged.
- Interpret the result as registered-anchor overlap/coverage under the frozen scorer.
- Do not infer complete evidence exposure at B50, even if later checks happen to show it.
- A separate full-span and semantic-obligation audit is needed before using the stronger phrase.
- This code-level finding alone does not show that rank 35 is numerically wrong or that the returned historical answer is wrong. Neither was recomputed here.

New measurement layers must be explicit:

1. EntryHit: a returned unit intersects the registered source unit.
2. AnchorHit: a returned unit intersects the registered anchor.
3. SpanCover: the union of returned intervals covers an entire declared evidence span.
4. BindingRecover: the exposed material licenses actor, target, evidence and modality assignments.
5. WarrantCheck: the returned claim and its uncertainty follow under the declared source/inference contract, including relevant challenges.
6. ContinuationCheck: retained state and lawful operations support a later inquiry or selective revision.

There may be alternative sufficient carrier families. Do not require one author's favorite pair of anchors when a different source-grounded route answers the same question. More evidence may introduce a defeater, so warrant is not assumed monotone in the number of exposed snippets.

## 4. New candidate core: preserve the capacity to continue, not just today's answer

Candidate hypothesis, not an established result:

> A transformation can preserve the current answer, text objects and even currently used citations while altering the distinctions needed to formulate, investigate or revise a later source-grounded inquiry. The relevant preservation obligation depends on the admissible continuation family and access contract, not only the current output.

The object of comparison is a research checkpoint with an externally defined source history and a retained representation. Do not define the reference history as whatever a representation happens to retain.

Let h be the full reference history used only by the evaluator, z = Phi(h) the complete retained interface, K the source/evidence/access contract, and u a declared continuation (possibly an adaptive protocol rather than a fixed query). W_K(h,u) is a set of source-licensed informative outcomes, not necessarily one canonical historical interpretation.

A bounded separation witness can have:

    Phi(h1) = Phi(h2)
    CurrentAnswer(h1) = CurrentAnswer(h2)
    W_K(h1,u) and W_K(h2,u) are disjoint

provided the consumer receives the same continuation information and no permitted observation can resolve the ambiguity before the decision under test. Then no deterministic consumer of that identical information can produce an informative output correct for both histories. This is an elementary determinacy argument, NOT a new theorem.

Important qualifications:

- If K permits reopening a distinguishing source, the claim may become increased cost or loss under a budget, not information-theoretic impossibility.
- One failed action sequence does not prove failure of every legal policy. Report fixed-policy results separately from finite-domain reachability or uniform-policy results.
- Safe abstention can remain possible. Failure to make informative progress is not necessarily an unsafe answer.
- A transition staying inside a defined state space proves almost nothing about sufficiency. The earlier proposed condition F(S,a,R) in S_R is not accepted as a preservation theorem.
- If transformations preserve every future operation on the same domain with compatible interfaces, their composition preserves those operations. Do not claim a composition paradox. Agreement only on the current task is the weaker premise worth testing.

Useful finite-theory target: define source-grounded, costed simulation of admissible inquiry operations, allowing native operations to be implemented by different lawful paths. Prove preservation only for the specified domain, horizon and observable obligations. Any reduction to established simulation, sufficient statistics, or view determinacy must be acknowledged.

## 5. What counts as a genuine continuation experiment

An episode should contain a historically meaningful root question, a source-grounded trigger, at least two admissible follow-up branches, a later evidence/status event, and a checkable effect on which conclusions can be maintained, revised or left unresolved.

The specific future branch is not supplied to the representation constructor. The evaluator freezes branch grammar, evidence-release rules, source scopes and admissibility conditions beforehand. Branch availability is generated from evidence visible at that point, not from a hidden answer or an instruction telling the agent which missing field to request.

Equivalent successful paths receive credit. We do not require agents to reproduce an author's exact chain of questions. An agent asking more questions is not automatically more successful: questions must identify a live source-grounded distinction and have an admissible route to further evidence or a justified stopping condition.

Illustrative bounded sequence, not a new historical result:

    locate a variant -> identify a revision feature -> trace responsible hand/source
    -> encounter uncertainty -> inspect a related witness -> revise only affected claims

A scripted list of all six tasks given in advance is a joint static-query benchmark, not the intended discovery experiment.

## 6. Three linked experimental modules

### C1. Current-equivalent / continuation-separating test

Construct or identify two checkpoint representations that yield the same present task answer. Apply the same independently specified next-question or source-status event. Test continuation with fixed reference policies first.

Required comparators:

- mature native representation and native pointer/URI/query route;
- ordinary source-text plus stable links and provenance/dependency records;
- tested transformed state with exactly declared side information;
- lawful source-reopening route, explicitly charged;
- source-grounded repair and matched wrong-binding control;
- harmless transformation/event control.

A constructed twin remains controlled mechanism evidence. A real publication/export transformation can support workflow evidence. Do not invent an editorial retraction merely because it would produce a useful effect.

### C2. Endogenous question branching and selective revision

A prior finding exposes multiple source-supported next questions. Test whether the representation preserves the trigger, identity and unresolved dependency needed to recognize and pursue those questions. Later evidence must affect only the conclusions it actually bears on.

Measure at least: eligible branch reached; source-bound referent maintained; necessary evidence recovered; unsupported commitment; correct unresolved outcome; missed warranted revision; collateral revision of unaffected claims; source reopenings and exposure cost.

Do not use the same LLM as task generator, historical gold, outcome scorer and final arbiter. LLMs may propose questions or try to break the protocol. Primary documentary checks use source spans, native annotations and explicit rules. Author-only interpretive assumptions are declared and tested against alternatives; ambiguity remains ambiguity. A second human is not conjured into the design.

### C3. Repeated transformation and delayed dependence

At successive checkpoints, serialize/export/normalize/retrieve under a declared interface, then allow a later query or correction whose need was not the optimization target of the earlier transformation.

Vary both horizon and the delay until a retained distinction is required. Include invariant-preserving recoding and strong full-native/reopening controls. Record the first point at which a required distinction becomes inaccessible, ambiguous, or dependent on source reopening.

The primary issue is not the number of turns. It is whether successive transformations preserve the ability to reuse and revise the accumulated evidence structure.

C1-C3 share one source registry, episode representation and evaluator. They are not a mandate to build three unrelated projects or increase sample counts arbitrarily.

## 7. Matched information, policy and cost

Cross representation with at least one native/carrier-aware policy and one baseline policy wherever operations are support-feasible. Hold the decision contract and source-release schedule fixed. Distinguish missing operation support from a zero-accuracy observation.

Native source reopening and parent-entry expansion are essential comparators: Great Desert's rank disparity must not be advertised as an infrastructure failure if an ordinary returned source link exposes the whole entry cheaply. Such a recovery is a valid null or cost finding.

Report source bytes/tokens, unique source spans, navigation/lookup calls, retained payload, preprocessing/index cost, and reopenings. Equal chunk count is not equal evidence or computational cost. Do not match away precisely the information distinction under study; instead state its footprint and compare cost frontiers.

Report both:

- capability under a bounded, enumerated action/evidence domain when feasible;
- realized performance of named policies.

A lookup table constructed from all test answers is not an admissible repair unless its construction and test-time availability are charged and the claim is correspondingly restricted.

## 8. Source and holdout audit

Frankenstein and Whitman are NOT fresh independent validation objects for this project. Both have already shaped its vocabulary and mechanisms.

- R2 cross-task protocol explicitly lists Frankenstein C01-C05 and C16-C18 as opened development data. It also selects additional chunks. Their later execution/exposure must be audited before assigning any new holdout label.
- R1 consumed the complete pinned Whitman relation inventory of 1,444 records. A new task on that same file is a new test on exposed data, not untouched-corpus replication.
- Faust is an independent upstream infrastructure, but its already examined graph stages are not an unobserved same-mechanism test.

Proposed roles:

- Yule-Cordier: source-grounded proposition/stance/revision development and historical importance.
- Frankenstein: continuation from collation to documentary provenance; native replay is the baseline.
- Whitman: continuation under encoded uncertainty and cross-file relations; distinguish archive-state recovery from genetic interpretation.
- Faust: conflict/status/composition pressure test, without forcing semantic equivalence to Yule-Cordier.

A holdout needs a frozen source/version/episode-group exposure ledger, not just a different filename or a renamed protocol. Select uninspected groups or a separately justified object only after the mechanism and task contract are fixed. Group by shared witnesses, source episodes and dependencies; do not treat turns, chunks, graph edges or model calls as independent replications.

A mechanism may be selected because source structure makes it testable, but not because its observed outcome is favorable. Count eligible null, inaccessible, malformed and unresolved cases.

## 9. Historical validity and instrument repair

The old 45-call historical audit remains BOUNDED_PARTIAL with no successful model-separated replication. It must not be relabelled after improving page labels or quote checking.

A new instrument should separately report:

    execution -> JSON/schema -> source locator -> quote alignment
    -> cross-field coherence -> semantic support -> uncertainty handling

One invalid quote must not silently turn every other component into semantic disagreement. Conversely, a quote copied perfectly can accompany a reversed or unsupported relation. A corrected future evaluator requires new versioning, adversarial unit tests and its own prospectively frozen evaluation. It is not a retroactive pass for Gate II.

The source-readings remain at their actual authority. New continuation claims dependent on contested readings must either survive the admissible alternative readings or be explicitly conditional. Resource scarcity does not prove validity; it constrains the claim and the way validation is organized.

## 10. Primary-literature and portfolio boundary screen

This is an initial scope screen, not a completed novelty review.

- Fan, Geerts and Zheng, View determinacy for preserving selected information in data transformations (2012), DOI 10.1016/j.is.2011.09.001: fixed-query preservation/invertibility are prior art.
- de Kleer, An assumption-based TMS (1986), DOI 10.1016/0004-3702(86)90080-9: maintaining assumptions, alternatives and context-sensitive reasoning is prior art.
- Predictive Representations of State (NIPS 2001), https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2001/hash/1e4d36177d71bbb3558e43af9577d70e-Abstract.html: representing history for future action-conditional observations is prior art.
- CRMinf 1.2.1, https://cidoc-crm.org/extensions/crminf/html/CRMinf_v1.2.1.html: inference, provenance assessment, meaning comprehension and belief adoption already have explicit modeling constructs.

Consequently, 'future matters', 'keep provenance', 'store unresolved states', 'truth maintenance', a state-transition equation, or a history-equivalence quotient cannot be the sole novelty.

The possible advance to establish is a nontrivial source-grounded preservation boundary across real representation transformations: current-output adequacy versus continued ability to identify questions, recover evidence, preserve uncertainty and selectively revise conclusions, with native-baseline comparisons and a useful constructive condition or repair. Whether that advance exists remains open.

The prior RESEARCH_PLAN explicitly excludes importing the author's earlier EEQ or writable-state theorems as new DH contributions. Keep the representation/inquiry problem connected to the portfolio, but do not rename existing identification, control or update-invariance results.
