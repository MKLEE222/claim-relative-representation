## Purpose

Close the currently open chain:

\[
representation / navigation
\rightarrow
active evidence set
\rightarrow
epistemic relation
\rightarrow

\]

The experiment uses the paper-money case because PM01-PM03 are page-image verified.

## Frozen claim

**C_PM_MATERIAL_USE**

> Polo's identification of mulberry bark as material used for paper-money is usable as evidence for Yuan paper-money practice under the supplied editorial record.

This is a claim about evidential usability under the supplied record, not a universal assertion of historical truth.

## Fixed evidence universe

Same in both conditions:

- Project Gutenberg V1 + V2;
- frozen native hashes;
- PM01 and PM02 are fixed context;
- H0 background only;
- no external scholarship.

## Fixed retrieval budget

Each condition exposes exactly 12 retrieved candidate windows in addition to fixed PM01/PM02 context.

Candidate-window size and tokenization follow the frozen access experiments.

## Condition C1 - generic retrieval

Uses the already-frozen generic seed-similarity policy on the declared V1+V2 collection.

Known construction property from the pre-existing retrieval experiment:
PM03 is not in the top 20 and therefore not in the top-12 adjudication packet.

## Condition C2 - sequence-guided domain chain

Uses the already-frozen sequence-guided domain-chain policy.

Known construction property from the pre-existing retrieval experiment:
the candidate set has 12 windows and PM03 is target ordinal 1.

## Why this is not post-hoc retrieval tuning

Both retrieval policies and their target ranks were established before this warrant-closure protocol.

This experiment does not retune either policy.

It tests a downstream consequence of already observed access differences.

## Blinding

The adjudicator receives:
- the frozen claim;
- PM01 exact excerpt;
- PM02 exact excerpt;
- 12 candidate windows;
- Stage-A documentary facts recoverable from the supplied packet;
- the Stage-B manual.

The adjudicator does not receive:
- policy name;
- C1/C2 interpretation;
- target rank;
- PM03 identifier;
- author provisional decision;
- expected direction;
- prior experiment reports.

Packet order is independently permuted by a fixed, preregistered seed.

## Adjudication outputs

For each condition:

1. E_* relation set relevant to C_PM_MATERIAL_USE;
2. warrant state: RETAIN / REVISE / DEFER / WITHHOLD;
3. one-sentence source rationale;
4. unresolved dependency if applicable.

## Primary consequence test

A representation/navigation consequence exists if the two anonymous packets receive different warrant states under the same claim, evidence universe, background contract, and candidate budget.

Secondary:
difference in E_* relation set.

## Claim ceiling

A positive result supports:

> task-adapted access can alter the active evidence state sufficiently to change a human adjudication of claim entitlement under a fixed scholarly contract.

It does not establish:
- historical truth about Yuan currency;
- universal effects across DH;
- that sequence-guided retrieval is globally superior.

## Independence requirement

No LLM/self-recode substitutes for the independent adjudicator.
