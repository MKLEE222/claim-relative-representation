## 8. Claim ceiling

Module N remains exposed development evidence.

It supports:
- mechanism-level non-substitutability for the registered obligations;
- a capability-vector interpretation rather than a scalar representation score;
- a stronger bridge between representation mechanics and distinct scholarly actions.

It does not support:
- universal necessity of all obligations for all humanities tasks;
- unseen-corpus generalization;
- a fresh independent dynamic confirmation;
- cross-event-family generality.

The remaining empirical burden is therefore narrower:

1. choose a small preregistered subset of obligations for a fresh independent eligible holdout;
2. obtain at least one prospective eligible dynamic trajectory population;
3. test the obligation logic on a second, non-temporal humanities event family.
