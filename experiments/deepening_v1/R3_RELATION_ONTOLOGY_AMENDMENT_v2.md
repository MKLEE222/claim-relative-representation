# R3 relation ontology amendment v2

Date: 2026-09-27
Status: frozen before full inventory coding; motivated by source inspection, not by representation outcomes.

## Amendment

Add:

**FACTUAL_CORRECTION**

A later editorial act explicitly rejects, replaces, or corrects a factual/descriptive proposition in the earlier edition, where the target is not itself an earlier criticism.

Examples include:
- an earlier statement that an inscription was known later being denied/corrected;
- an earlier linguistic/geographical derivation being stated to be inaccurate;
- a previously printed factual form being corrected when the issue is not merely a variant reading.

## Distinctions

FACTUAL_CORRECTION != CORRECTION_OF_PRIOR_CRITICISM

The latter requires:
earlier actor A criticizes proposition P;
later actor B explicitly attacks/corrects A's criticism.

FACTUAL_CORRECTION targets the earlier edition's proposition directly.

FACTUAL_CORRECTION != TEXTUAL_UPDATE

TEXTUAL_UPDATE concerns the reading/translation/witness form itself.
FACTUAL_CORRECTION concerns what the edition asserts about the world, history, language, chronology, identification, or evidence.

A single act may carry FACTUAL_CORRECTION plus:
- IDENTIFICATION_UPDATE;
- ATTRIBUTION_UPDATE;
- QUALIFICATION;
where the source explicitly supports both.
