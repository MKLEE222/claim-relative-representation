# Current bounded scientific answer

The mother problem now has the following bounded answer:

> What must survive representational change is not a fixed maximal inventory of fields or metadata. For a declared scholarly task and historical entry state, a sufficient source-grounded carrier system must remain available or lawfully recoverable; the access representation must expose enough of that carrier system, under the declared workflow and inspection regime, for the task-relevant epistemic relations and statuses to be determined and composed. Content survival, target discovery, carrier completeness, relation determinacy, provenance/status fidelity and downstream executability are distinct conditions.

A compact indexing is:

[
Researchability(R,	au,S,K,pi;B)
]

where:

- (R) = representation;
- (	au) = scholarly task;
- (S) = research entry state;
- (K) = admissible source/carrier contract;
- (pi) = transformation/access workflow, including unitization/chunking and ranking/navigation;
- (B) = declared inspection budget for practical discovery/exposure claims.

For pure logical determinacy, (B) may be irrelevant.
For practical researchability, (B) is part of the evaluation contract.
