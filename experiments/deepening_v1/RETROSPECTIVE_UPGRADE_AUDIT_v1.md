# Retrospective Upgrade Audit v1

Date: 2026-09-27
Status: research-priority audit after R3-first reframing; old results keep their original authority.

## U1 — Research entry state belongs in adequacy

Earlier evidence already showed that fixed-endpoint relation tasks can hide discovery failure. In the paper-money interaction pilot, once PM01/PM02/PM03 were supplied, access/context interventions could no longer test whether the later witness could actually be found. The task-entry-state ledger already separates seed/object discovery, post-discovery sequence reconstruction, and fixed-endpoint relation analysis.

Upgrade: distinguish a representation that answers a question after discovery has been solved from one that supports the research path from a realistic starting state.

Candidate object: Researchability(R, tau, K, E0), where E0 is the research entry state.

R3 implication: for selected genealogies compare at least RELATION_KNOWN and HISTORICAL_START while holding the historical target fixed.

## U2 — Carrier redundancy and basis substitution are substantive

Experiment 1A found residual lawful cues after one-coordinate removal: attribution 7/10, temporal order 5/10, evidence relation 7/10, claim binding 7/10. The transformation-realism audit also showed that Gutenberg plaintext still carries headings, signatures, chronology, note structure, and backward pointers.

Recovery Contract v2 already names this basis substitution. The missed opportunity was treating it mainly as leakage/design repair.

Upgrade: important scholarly relations can be multiply carried. A relation may survive loss of one explicit field through actor names, signatures, note attachment, page pointers, section chronology, lexical reporting constructions, or another source-grounded basis.

R3 implication: build relation-carrier maps and test carrier substitution before claiming information loss.

## U3 — Output equivalence is weaker than recovery-profile equivalence

Task Contract v2 already distinguishes output equivalence from profile equivalence. Recovery Contract v2 records recoverability class, minimum evidence boundary, basis, assumption burden, residual uncertainty, and operational cost.

Experiment 1B was superseded because depth alone was too narrow, not because the full profile was invalid.

Upgrade: two representations may yield the same historical answer while requiring very different reconstruction paths. This is a substantive researchability difference even without binary task failure.

R3 implication: restore recovery depth only as one coordinate of the full profile. Report E/R/Q/U, D0-D4, lawful basis family, ambiguity, and mechanically observable traversal/exposure counts. Do not equate these with human effort.

## U4 — Typed mediation is a documentary research-path layer

Paper money, Kinsay, and coal already instantiated three different lawful routes: cited-source + sequence; referent alias + sequence; local note attachment.

Upgrade: reinterpret these not as competing retrieval tricks but as recognizable scholarly practices: citation chaining, name/referent reconciliation, note following, chronological traversal, and explicit pointer following.

R3 implication: for each genealogy record the lawful documentary path from realistic entry state to later intervention, then ask whether a representation preserves, lengthens, substitutes, ambiguates, or breaks that path.

## U5 — Significant-properties research is a missing nearest neighbor

Digital-preservation work has long asked which characteristics must be preserved so a digital object remains accessible, usable, meaningful, and acceptable as evidence. The paper must not claim novelty for asking what must be preserved for future use.

Sharper gap: this project can test which source-bound scholarly relations remain explicit or reconstructible for a declared historical research operation from a declared entry state, expose alternative carriers, distinguish output from profile equivalence, and produce recovery/separation/repair evidence.

## U6 — N/W/C/R can become an evidence architecture

The ecological-validity contract already separates Native, Workflow, Controlled, and Reference representations. This should be promoted from bookkeeping into triangulation.

For a load-bearing R3 claim, state which authority establishes it: a naturally existing representation, a documented workflow replay, a controlled separation/twin, and/or source-verified reference. Do not pool heterogeneous authorities into one score.

## Revised central direction

The stronger candidate object is no longer only Adequacy(R,tau,K). It is task- and entry-state-sensitive researchability plus a relation-level recovery profile.

The desired result is not 'some metadata fields matter'. It is: a scholarly relation may survive representational change through redundant carriers, remain answer-equivalent while becoming harder to reconstruct, or become undiscoverable from a realistic research entry state; these states can be distinguished and, in some cases, repaired with source-bound evidence.
