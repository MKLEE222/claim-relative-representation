# R3 Primary Programme v3 — Researchability and Editorial Knowledge History

Date: 2026-09-27
Status: ACTIVE; extends R3_PRIMARY_PROGRAM v2 after retrospective upgrade audit.

## Primary humanistic object

Editorial knowledge history remains primary. The new emphasis is that an edition supports scholarship not only by containing text but by making the history of scholarly responsibility discoverable and reconstructible from realistic research starting points.

## Level H — historical result

Establish what kinds of intervention the 1920 Addenda performs, by whom, on which prior claims, and with what consequences for identification, attribution, chronology, textual reading, material culture, and other historical questions.

## Level R — researchability result

For a historical task and starting state, determine whether required relations are explicit, recoverable by an alternative carrier, recoverable only at a deeper documentary boundary, ambiguous, or unavailable.

## R3-F — carrier resilience

Select verified acts across at least five intervention families. For each load-bearing relation enumerate native carrier bases, test lawful substitution, and identify basis exhaustion only where all registered alternatives are removed or become ambiguous.

## R3-G — entry-state study

Compare at least RELATION_KNOWN, where relevant endpoints are supplied, with HISTORICAL_START, where an earlier locus/claim is supplied and later treatment must be discovered. Hold the historical target constant.

## R3-H — recoverability profile

Record recoverability class, minimum evidence boundary, carrier basis family, assumption burden, residual ambiguity, and mechanically observed exposure/traversal vector. Report output equivalence separately from profile equivalence.

## R3-I — source-bound repair

For a supported separation or burden increase, test correct bridge, matched wrong bridge, unaffected task, and explicit information footprint.

## Inventory relation — corrected after frame-scope audit

The legacy 223-entry object is **not** the denominator for whole-book historical or prevalence claims.

It remains only the frozen retrieval universe for experiments that were already executed on those exact intervals. Its reproducibility is preserved, but it is not promoted into a validated population of retrospective interventions.

Any new denominator-bearing humanities result must use a separately versioned hierarchical frame that distinguishes at minimum:

- publication section;
- top-level Addenda entry;
- named or signed contribution;
- page-addressed subnote;
- embedded quotation or transmitted source passage;
- proposition-level intervention target.

Nested units are not pooled as equivalent top-level observations. Bibliography, index, Gutenberg wrapper material, and other publication sections may be historically relevant but may not silently inherit the identity of the preceding Addenda entry.

No replacement denominator is authorized until the hierarchical frame has been source-audited and frozen. Existing legacy relation labels, retrieval ranks, seeds, negative LLM results, and completed experimental objects remain unchanged.
