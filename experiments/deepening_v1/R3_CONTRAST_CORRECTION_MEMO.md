## Main correction

The first-pass B02 label ATTRIBUTION_UPDATE on YC1920E-0030 was too strong.

The earlier 1903 Pashai note already says that Polo likely did not personally visit the countries and was probably reproducing Mongol/hearsay information.

Thus 1920 Stein does not introduce the source-attribution thesis. He corroborates and sharpens it.

This is exactly the kind of overstatement the R3 historical study must prevent.

## New rule

All UPDATE/CORRECTION labels now require earlier-layer contrast.

The transfer panel is revised before any representation outcome is opened.

## Revised attribution transfer

YC1920E-0049 is the first source-order case currently verified to add a previously unstated source-derivation assignment: Stein says Polo's desert-spirit account was a reflex of local folklore he must have heard on the spot.

The 1903 note already supplies the broad comparative folklore context, so the 1920 act is also corroborative; the new element is the explicit source derivation attributed to Polo's report.

## Broader humanistic consequence

The 1920 Addenda must be analyzed as a mixture of:
- genuine revision;
- new assignment;
- corroboration of existing editorial knowledge;
- additive evidence;
- qualification;
- contestation.

Counting all later scholarship as "change" would misdescribe the edition's knowledge history.
