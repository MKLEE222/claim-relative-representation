The earlier ontology distinguished:
- criticism;
- correction of prior criticism;

but had no label for an Addenda act that directly corrects a factual/descriptive claim in the earlier edition.

This gap was discovered before complete inventory counts were produced and has now been frozen in R3_RELATION_ONTOLOGY_AMENDMENT_v2.md.
