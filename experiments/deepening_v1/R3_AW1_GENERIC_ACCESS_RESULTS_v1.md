## Main scientific result

R3-AW1 separates three states that would be conflated by a binary preserved/lost account:

1. relation-bearing text can survive a real transformation;
2. a frozen workflow can make some later treatments readily discoverable from a historical start;
3. another treatment in the same representation can remain practically unsurfaced under the same workflow.

Thus:

[
content/relation recoverability 
eq discoverability from a historical entry state
]

and the difference is empirically instantiated inside one native digital object under one pre-existing access policy.

The result is not a representation leaderboard.

It supports the stronger structured view already suggested by the capability audit:
- scope inclusion;
- discoverability;
- relation determinacy;
- provenance/responsibility;
- uncertainty fidelity;
- composability

are separable research-support capabilities.

## Relation to the controlled R3 mechanism

The controlled proposition-binding experiments showed that removing stance-to-target, role/commitment, or evidence-to-proposition bindings can make an inquiry underdetermined.

The natural Project Gutenberg check then showed that plain text itself does not instantiate those controlled binding deletions because alternative textual carriers remain.

R3-AW1 now adds an ecological workflow result:
even when the proposition relation remains recoverable in the object, a realistic research-access path can alter whether the later treatment is surfaced from the historical starting state.

Therefore the controlled mechanism and ecological workflow result are complementary, not identical:

- CONTROLLED: binding determinacy under declared projections;
- NATURAL OBJECT: alternative textual carriers preserve direct recoverability;
- WORKFLOW: access/discovery burden varies sharply across inquiries.
