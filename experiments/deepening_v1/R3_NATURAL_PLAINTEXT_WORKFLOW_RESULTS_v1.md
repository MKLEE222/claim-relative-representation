# R3 Natural Plain-Text Workflow Check v1

Date: 2026-09-27
Status: EXECUTED NEGATIVE / BOUNDED NATURAL-WORKFLOW RESULT

## Question

Does the naturally available Project Gutenberg UTF-8 representation of the Yule-Cordier material reproduce the proposition-binding attrition observed in the controlled R3 projections?

This check is intentionally stronger than asking whether rich print structure is reduced. The relevant question is whether the five frozen historical inquiries cease to be determinable from the naturally published plain-text witnesses because the required proposition-level carriers are absent.

## Workflow

Natural representation under test:

printed Yule-Cordier / Cordier objects
-> Project Gutenberg digital transcription / assembly
-> UTF-8 plain-text research witness

No synthetic deletion operator is applied in this check.

The registered Gutenberg objects are the same source family already used by the project:
- 1903 Volume I: pg10636.txt
- 1920 Notes and Addenda: pg12410.txt

The existing source audit has already established that these digital witnesses preserve substantial edition, note, signature, page-pointer, and cross-layer cues. This check asks whether those retained textual carriers are sufficient for the five frozen R3 inquiries at the level of source inspection.

## Frozen inquiries checked

### R3Q-PASH-STANCE

Observed in the Gutenberg witness:
- the earlier source-critical/non-eyewitness position is present;
- Stein's explicit agreement with that position is present;
- the later route criticism and qualified alternative are separately expressed.

Disposition: REQUIRED DISTINCTION REMAINS TEXTUALLY RECOVERABLE.

### R3Q-ARBR-COMMIT

Disposition: RESPONSIBILITY/ADOPTION DISTINCTION REMAINS TEXTUALLY RECOVERABLE.

### R3Q-DES-EVIDENCE

Disposition: THE TWO EVIDENCE-TO-PROPOSITION ASSIGNMENTS REMAIN TEXTUALLY RECOVERABLE.

### R3Q-TUN-CONTROVERSY

Disposition: CONTROVERSY STRUCTURE REMAINS TEXTUALLY RECOVERABLE.

### R3Q-URM-ERRATUM

- the explicit substitution from "found" to "founded" remains visible.

Disposition: CONTROL REMAINS DETERMINATE.

## Result

The natural Gutenberg plain-text representation does **not** reproduce the controlled proposition-binding failures.

For all five frozen inquiries, the presently inspected plain-text witnesses retain enough lexical, attributional, pointer, sequencing, and local-context carriers for the registered distinctions to remain recoverable by close reading.

Therefore:

NATURAL_GUTENBERG_PLAINTEXT_BINDING_ATTRITION = NOT OBSERVED

This is a negative result for the strong natural-loss hypothesis.

## Scientific consequence

The controlled R3 separation results remain valid as information-obligation demonstrations, but they may not be redescribed as evidence that plain text itself naturally removes those bindings.

The claim ceiling is now stricter:

> proposition-level bindings can be necessary under controlled projections, while a real plain-text transformation may preserve alternative textual carriers that keep the historical inquiry recoverable.

This directly supports the carrier-system view: explicit structured edges are not the only lawful carrier family.

## What this closes

- rejects the naive claim that Project Gutenberg plain text is a natural witness of attribution/binding loss;
- supplies the first direct natural-workflow check at proposition level;
- converts one open possibility into a bounded null rather than leaving it speculative.

## What remains open

The mother problem still requires a realistic downstream workflow in which proposition-level recoverability changes naturally, if the paper is to make a positive ecological attrition claim.

The next workflow must therefore be selected independently of outcome and must act on a real research-facing representation process, for example:
- corpus segmentation/chunking;
- field extraction into a search index;
- API/export projection;
- retrieval-window construction.

The next experiment must freeze:
1. workflow implementation or documented default;
2. entry state;
3. admissible carriers before output;
4. proposition-level inquiry;
5. correct repair and matched wrong repair if a separation occurs.

A null under the next workflow is also admissible and must be retained.
