Candidate: an executable audit and limited repair method that ties a concrete scholarly question to (a) source-licensed bindings, (b) transformations actually applied by a scholarly project, (c) permitted reconstruction operations, and (d) independently checkable recovery or separation evidence.

## 3. Three claims requiring different evidence

### Decoder recovery

A particular permitted decoder D recovers the declared task answer Q from the exposed representation. Demonstrate actual execution on the registered population. Failure of D alone does not establish information loss.

### Projection separation

For a named projection P and complete consumer-visible input (including side information K), exhibit x and x' such that P(x)=P(x') and Q(x) differs from Q(x'). A native record-local collision proves only record-local non-determinacy. A whole-document claim requires equality of the whole exposed document, group structure, IDs, ordering, metadata and authorized auxiliary resources. Constructed twins must be labelled controlled schema/completion witnesses, not naturally observed historical alternatives.

### Interpretive consequence

A documentary difference affects an interpretation under a separately justified interpretation policy. State the mapping assumptions, alternatives and sensitivity. Do not equate admissibility, descriptive testimony, endorsed truth, undefeated support, or membership of a grounded extension.

No one evidence type substitutes for the others.

## 4. Work package R1: source-bound recovery and repair

### Populations

Use the complete already-inspected pinned Whitman relation list and Faust temporal-assertion inventory as development/diagnostic populations, not fresh holdouts. Keep every eligible record and every malformed or unsupported case in the denominator. Preserve all source elements per assertion, their locators, grouping, and order when semantically relevant.

### Consumers

Whitman: first specify a record-local endpoint consumer, then a separate whole-document printed-locus-to-endpoint-set consumer. Certainty recovery is a separate target. Record-local ambiguity cannot silently support a whole-document impossibility claim.

Faust: distinguish source-work sets, source-plus-locator bundles, and graph-structure tasks. Preserve the full set of sources for each assertion. Distinguish assertion occurrences from distinct edges and edge-level aggregated provenance from assertion-level provenance.

### Comparators

1. Native full-source consumer using the project's exposed binding mechanisms.
2. Straightforward standard query/URI-resolution or relational-join implementation supplied the same lawful inputs.
3. Frozen projection without repair.
4. The proposed source-bound repair mechanism, limited to registered source-side routes.
5. Wrong-binding or wrong-source repair, preserving superficial format, as a controlled negative control.
6. Source-complete reopening as an explicitly charged upper-information control, not a competitor with the same information budget.

Run projections on actual data before invoking the consumer. The consumer must not receive hidden filenames, directory IDs, group indices, original-object handles, evaluation rows, target labels, or a lookup learned from the same test outputs unless these are explicitly retained and charged.

### Certificates

A recovery certificate records source identity, the exact projection, task input, allowed side information, decoder version, reconstruction trace, complete output and comparison with a separately implemented native reference query.

A failure witness records complete equality of the retained inputs and the distinct required outputs. Include all keys actually available; do not test MS_LOCUS alone while claiming a result about PRINT_LOCUS+MS_LOCUS+CERTAINTY.

A lack of a collision is not a proof of sufficiency. A source-labelled finite lookup table is not an available inverse unless its construction and test-time accessibility are explicitly part of the method.

### Minimality

Distinguish inclusion-minimal carrier sets from minimum-cost sets. Minimality is relative to the named carrier vocabulary, permitted decoder family and data/completion domain. For every supported strict-subset failure, retain a valid separation witness or a stated limitation to the registered decoder family. Unknown cases remain unknown. Do not print a predefined minimum as an empirical discovery.

### Outcomes

Exact endpoint/source-set recovery; false added and omitted bindings; valid unresolved outputs; false certificates; source accesses; exposed payload; repair footprint; correct-repair versus wrong-repair difference. Minimality and information-theoretic statements require the corresponding proof obligations, not an accuracy threshold.

## 5. Work package R2: native normalization and cross-task preservation

### Fidelity gate

Identify and pin the actual Frankenstein source tokenizer, normalizer, alignment and output-processing code and dependencies. First reproduce the native output or document the exact reason it cannot be reproduced. Separate conditional grouping recovery on supplied app boundaries from end-to-end alignment from witness inputs. Neither test substitutes for the other.

Do not derive independent predictions by copying rdgGrp membership or its n attribute from the evaluation output. Native grouping is the target for a pipeline-reproduction task, not independent historical truth.

### Comparators

- Raw serialized equality (historical diagnostic baseline).
- Explicitly specified generic normalization.
- Original project's native normalization and alignment pipeline.
- Native normalized representation with its source pointers/normalization map.
- Proposed limited source-bound relation repair.

### Tasks

Recover the native witness partition; locate the corresponding source token/span; recover a declared source-documentary coordinate; and preserve the registered distinction between absence, deletion, addition and normalization when the native data supports it. Unsupported task constructs are N/A, not fabricated labels.

### Required analyses

Report false splitting and false merging separately. Audit cases where ordinary normalization removes accidental markup differences, and cases where it merges distinctions needed by another task. Report complete source mapping and the cost of auxiliary normalization/lookup information. The comparator may retain useful mappings; richer background is not forbidden, only accounted for.

Expand beyond C16-C18 through a metadata-based, versioned chunk inventory. C16-C18 and previously inspected targets remain development. Unopened chunks must receive a documented exposure ledger and frozen task/decoder versions before target evaluation. Within-book chunks are transfer units, not independent books or infrastructures. Do not select a new chunk because it has a large desired mismatch count.

### Hypotheses

Test, do not assume, whether most old mismatches disappear under the native baseline, whether source mapping restores other tasks, and whether a smaller source-bound repair can preserve a joint task set. Complete recovery by the native pipeline is a valid result; it defeats a necessity claim about one particular storage field.

## 6. Work package R3: historical chain and controlled access consequences

### Historical question

How are a reported observation, a later criticism, and subsequent responses attributed, connected and revised across the Yule-Cordier documentary layers, and what parts of that editorial history remain reconstructible under actual digital representations?

Source hierarchy: printed witnesses, page-tied OCR, Gutenberg digital objects, derived alignments. The registry's 1871/1875 discovery files are not thereby fully page-verified. A page-verified historical passage does not automatically verify every expanded digital excerpt used in a packet.

### Episode inventory

Keep known paper-money, Kinsay and coal episodes as development/boundary cases. Enumerate later references and attached notes in a predeclared source range, apply structural eligibility before retrieval outcomes, and retain all screened, excluded, inaccessible, unresolved and no-linked-response-in-scope cases. Choose additional episodes for historical relevance and source structure, not the availability of a reversal. Exact ranges, source hashes and any limit on inspection must be committed before the prospective execution; this plan does not claim that inventory is already sealed.

### Intervention and policy separation

Use a crossed representation-carrier x recovery-policy design where each cell is support-feasible. Hold source wording, task and evaluation target fixed in a controlled representation comparison. Apply the same lexical policy and same carrier-aware policy to both representations without secret fallback access. If the operation cannot be instantiated, report that support stop rather than an artificial accuracy zero.

Natural version differences are historical observations, not randomized causal effects. Controlled carrier interventions are mechanism tests, not estimates of natural failure prevalence.

### Controls and budgets

Preserve a source-complete positive control; content-preserving serialization controls; correct bridge restoration; matched wrong-target bridge; non-relevant source addition; and boundary cases not requiring a later layer. Match or report actual exposed words/bytes and distinct documentary loci, as well as hop/lookup counts. Equal window/unit counts alone do not establish equal evidence budget. Word and navigation budgets are computational exposure proxies, not measured human effort.

### Outcomes

Recover correct actor, editorial layer, cited source, targeted passage, referent and explicit stance span; distinguish source assertion from its truth. Record task-specific answer sets, unresolved alternatives, evidence-path provenance, and recovery burden. Do not require a RETAIN-to-WITHHOLD reversal to count an episode as scientifically useful.

Interpretation-policy sensitivity can follow, but remains conditional and post-result where applicable. The original 45-call judge null remains closed; no new LLM run is part of this phase.

## 7. Prospective transfer, statistics and scope

Already opened data provide repair and discovery evidence. A new source family or truly unopened source-group split tests transfer only after the rule, available inputs and expected output types are frozen. Opening an archive, inspecting its content and evaluating its target are different exposure events and must be logged.

Use chapters/witness groups, manuscript groups or source files as dependency-aware reporting units. Report exact finite-population results and per-source distributions. Do not turn app units, reused edges, shared sources or the 45 model calls into independent humanistic replicates. Any uncertainty interval must state its sampling interpretation; a small purposive corpus does not license population prevalence claims.

Evaluate task-information adequacy separately from computational cost and from interpretive policy. Avoid a pooled cross-material leaderboard.

## 8. Buildable core rather than a new name

Implement a small consumer-bound audit core with native adapters and explicit contracts. It should (a) serialize exactly what a consumer may see, (b) run a permitted decoder, (c) verify the source binding of returned answers, (d) return a recoverability certificate or bounded separation witness, and (e) test a registered repair without changing the task or accessing evaluator-only data.

Its capability must be tested against ordinary full-input native/query methods. A report wrapper around known methods is not by itself an algorithmic innovation. The research value must be established through a newly characterized failure boundary, a transferable repair capability, or a nontrivial source-constrained preservation result supported by actual comparisons.

Candidate structural properties to investigate: representation-recoding invariance when binding information is unchanged; conditional preservation under composition of source-faithful transformations; and task-relative repair requirements. Ordinary function composition, partition separation and view-determinacy facts retain prior attribution. No theorem is claimed merely by listing these targets.

## 9. Execution order and exit criteria

First R1 native consumers and source-set correctness; then R2 native replay; then joint repair/composition tests; then R3 historical selection and crossed access experiments. Historical source transcription can proceed alongside the first two work packages.

Stop adding unrelated corpora. Within selected ecologies, increasing coverage and independent source groups is encouraged after the selector is fixed. Stop a particular minimality claim when the full projection or source premise cannot be certified; continue the project at its supported scope.

A strong manuscript requires actual native-baseline recovery, valid complete-input witnesses for claimed necessities, a source-bound repair that survives wrong-bridge and unrelated-task controls, and a historically substantive chapter that does not depend on the LLM null becoming positive. No green CI badge or percentage substitutes for these obligations.
