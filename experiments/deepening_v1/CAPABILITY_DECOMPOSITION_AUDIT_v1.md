# Research-support capability decomposition audit v1

Date: 2026-09-27
Status: retrospective synthesis of already executed results; not a new confirmatory experiment.

## Finding

The earlier carrier ladder HELD -> ACCESSIBLE -> RECOVERABLE -> COMPOSABLE is too linear for the evidence now available.

Executed cases separate at least five research-support capabilities.

## C1 — scope inclusion

Question: is the required evidence/relation inside the declared representation horizon at all?

Paper money current-object condition: PM03 is outside scope.

This is not retrieval failure and not relation ambiguity.

## C2 — discoverability from research entry state

Question: given a realistic seed/question E0 and lawful policy pi, can the relevant evidence be surfaced?

Paper money collection condition: PM03 is in scope but generic seed retrieval ranks it 5835/6033.

Kinsay later-layer condition: target is in the correct layer but generic retrieval gives score 0 and rank 304/482; a native referent bridge moves it to rank 4.

Thus inclusion does not imply discoverability.

## C3 — relation determinacy / recoverability

Question: once the relevant material is available, does the visible representation uniquely determine the required source-bound relation under the declared contract?

Scrivener: page + old reading uniquely localizes the correction; old reading alone leaves two candidates.

Whitman R1: selected reduced record projections produce same-input/different-MS_FILE collisions.

Thus discoverability of text does not imply relation determinacy.

## C4 — responsibility / provenance traceability

Question: can the researcher recover who is responsible for the assertion/connection and the source locus that licenses it?

Faust X3B preserves temporal order while removing per-relation source locator or source exposure.

R3 act segmentation shows why this is humanistically separate: Cordier transmits Laufer; Laufer corrects Bretschneider. Textual co-presence without actor-target binding does not preserve responsibility history.

## C5 — resolution-state fidelity

Question: does the representation preserve that scholarship itself is uncertain, contested, possible, or unresolved rather than coercing it into a stronger settled relation?

Whitman X2C: endpoint links remain exactly available while native certainty status is not preserved when certainty is removed.

R3 documentary ontology already contains source-explicit uncertainty markers.

Resolution-state fidelity is not equivalent to endpoint recoverability.

## C6 — composability

Question: can individually represented relations be jointly used for the downstream scholarly operation without contradiction or extra conflict policy?

Faust raw graph: 1089 source-qualified temp-pre assertions yield 38 reciprocal pairs, 20 nontrivial SCCs, and 612 edges inside conflict SCCs.

All assertions may be present and source-qualified while the raw set is not directly composable into one acyclic chronology.

Thus recoverability of individual relations does not imply joint composability.

## Consequence

Do not collapse these capabilities into one scalar quality score or a strict sequential ladder.

A representation can be:
- rich in scope but poor in discoverability;
- discoverable but non-determinate for a responsibility task;
- exact for endpoints but non-preserving for editorial certainty;
- source-qualified relation by relation but non-composable as a global chronology.

## Candidate research-support profile

For a task tau, representation R, contract K, and entry state E0, record a structured profile:

- S: scope inclusion;
- D: discoverability under a declared policy or navigation route;
- Q: relation determinacy/recoverability;
- P: responsibility/provenance traceability;
- U: resolution-state fidelity;
- C: composability for the declared downstream operation.

This is a candidate empirical decomposition, not a universal ontology of humanities research.

## R3 implication

R3 should not ask only whether a later intervention is present.

For each selected editorial genealogy ask separately:
- is the later act in the representation horizon?;
- can a scholar find it from the earlier locus/question?;
- once found, can actor/target/relation be uniquely reconstructed?;
- is uncertainty/disagreement preserved?;
- can the reconstructed acts be composed into a controversy trajectory without silently resolving conflict?

These questions are derived from the historical object and earlier executed failures, not introduced to force a positive result.
