## Result

All three primary controlled projections produced same-visible-projection / different-required-output witnesses.

### Pashai — target-specific stance

`P_STANCE_BAG` retains Stein, both earlier propositions, and the stance/modality bags but removes which stance targets which proposition.

Two completions share the same visible projection:
- A: corroboration -> source attribution; criticism -> route;
- B: corroboration -> route; criticism -> source attribution.

The required historical answer differs. Therefore the flattened stance bag does not determine R3Q-PASH-STANCE.

### Arbre Sec — commitment/adoption

`P_ROLE_BAG` retains Cordier, Houtum-Schindler, the cypress proposition, its presence in the entry, and Cordier's bibliographical reply, but removes proposer/transmitter/adopter binding.

One completion has Houtum-Schindler as commitment holder while Cordier transmits/responds; another adds Cordier adoption. The visible projection is identical, but the answer to whether Cordier adopts the cypress identification differs.

Therefore the role-flattened projection does not determine R3Q-ARBR-COMMIT.

### Great Desert — evidence binding

`P_EVIDENCE_BAG` retains both propositions and both evidence items but removes evidence-to-proposition edges.

Actual and swapped evidence bindings have identical visible bags and different answers to which evidence supports distance/marches versus local-folklore attribution.

Therefore the unbound evidence bag does not determine R3Q-DES-EVIDENCE.

### Urumtsi control

`P_TEXT_PAIR` retains `found` and explicit replacement `founded`. The simple erratum inquiry remains determined.

## Scientific interpretation

This is the first R3 result that isolates a proposition-level information obligation beyond target localization.

The result supports a narrow claim:

> preserving the participating textual/propositional items is not sufficient for these inquiries when the transformation removes the binding that connects stance, responsibility, or evidence to the proposition it qualifies.

## Claim ceiling

The result does not show that plain text is insufficient. The tested projections are controlled relational flattenings.

The alternate completions are separation constructions, not alternative historical claims.

No human difficulty, platform prevalence, or universal representation theorem follows.
