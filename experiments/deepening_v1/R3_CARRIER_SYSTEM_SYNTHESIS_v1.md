## Main finding

The same documentary carrier family supports different research-path structures across neighboring editorial entries.

Observed structures include:

1. SUBSTITUTION / REDUNDANCY
   lexical content can independently recover the later target even though a native pointer also exists.

2. COMPLEMENTARITY
   a coarse pointer narrows the documentary horizon while lexical content discriminates the correct entry; neither route alone yields the same exact first-candidate profile.

Therefore the empirical object is not the necessity of one metadata coordinate.

It is the source-grounded carrier system available to a task from a declared research entry state.

## Set-family interpretation

For a fixed task, recovery contract and entry state, represent sufficient carrier combinations as a family of minimal sets.

- multiple singleton sufficient sets -> substitution;
- a minimal sufficient set of cardinality > 1 -> complementarity;
- coexistence of singleton and multi-carrier sets -> redundant composed routes;
- no sufficient registered set -> basis exhaustion or unresolved state, subject to the stronger collision/separation requirements.

## Humanistic interpretation

The pattern mirrors ordinary editorial research:
- a backward page pointer may constrain where to look;
- wording, quoted propositions, names and referents distinguish which later intervention is relevant;
- actor/stance markers then establish responsibility and controversy state.

The edition's research support is therefore partly relational and procedural: it preserves routes through which a later scholar can reconstruct editorial knowledge history.

## Boundary

The six prospective cases come from three pointer groups in one corpus and are not six independent infrastructures.

No prevalence estimate is made.

TF-IDF is only a deterministic discrimination instrument.

The key contribution is mechanism differentiation, not an accuracy leaderboard.
