# External DH validation synthesis v1

Date: 2026-09-24

## Current result

Three independently designed DH infrastructures now instantiate three different boundaries of the task-relative representation framework.

### Frankenstein Variorum

Observed:
- strongly heterogeneous witness structures;
- a common collation apparatus with 900 app units and all five witnesses;
- selective carry-through of documentary markup.

Mechanism:
**representation restructuring can be task-enabling**.

Boundary:
the collation representation is not claimed to preserve every source-documentary property.

### Walt Whitman Archive

Observed:
- 1444 explicit manuscript/print links;
- 508 high-certainty and 936 low-certainty links;
- 52 relation groups mixing certainty levels.

Controlled projection:
all targets preserved while all per-link certainty states are removed.

Mechanism:
**relation availability is distinct from epistemic/editorial commitment**.

### Digital Faust Edition

Observed:
- 77 source-qualified temporal assertions;
- 22 source locators;
- pairwise, three-item, and four-item ordering constraints.

Controlled projections:
temporal item order can be preserved while source locator or relation-local provenance is removed.

Mechanism:
**temporal relation availability is distinct from auditable source provenance**.

## Combined implication

The external materials do not support one generic "rich representations are better" thesis.

Instead they support a sharper form:

\[
Adequacy(R,\tau,K)
\]

depends on the scholarly task and recovery contract.

A transformation may:
- enable one task;
- preserve another;
- remove a third task's entitlement;
within the same scholarly object.

## Why this matters for Yule-Cordier

The primary Yule-Cordier study can now be positioned as the place where these representation-level distinctions are connected to a humanistic warrant chain.

The external DH materials independently pressure-test the framework without relying on Yule-Cordier-specific case engineering.
