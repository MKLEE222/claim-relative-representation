# Validation synthesis v4 - typed mediation and minimal sufficient carriers

Date: 2026-09-24

## New structure

The project now distinguishes two questions that were previously conflated:

1. **Can a legal source-grounded mediation primitive be instantiated?**
2. **Conditional on support, which carrier coordinates are sufficient for the frozen scholarly task?**

This follows the project's prior experimental discipline that support feasibility precedes downstream complexity and that a target must remain fixed while representation coordinates are compared.

## Theoretical direction

The emerging representation object is not a scalar information score.

For task tau and contract K, the project seeks a family of minimal sufficient carrier sets:

\[
\mathcal{M}_{tau,K}
=
\{M:\ M\text{ is sufficient and no strict subset is sufficient}\}.
\]

Carrier substitution means this family need not be unique.

The mediation support gate determines which carrier families can legally be instantiated from the source before such sufficiency is evaluated.
