## Interpretation

This is direct external evidence that a real DH project can deliberately transform heterogeneous witness representations into a common task-facing representation for collation.

The result is important because it is the opposite of a one-directional "representation loss" story.

For the Frankenstein collation task, restructuring/flattening is part of the enabling workflow.

At the same time, X1A does **not** show global information preservation:
- the output is optimized for cross-witness alignment;
- not every native structural distinction is represented in the same form;
- no claim is made that the collation apparatus substitutes for the source editions for every scholarly task.

## Framework consequence

The external result supports:

\[
adequacy(R,\tau)
\]

rather than a global representation ranking.

A representation transformation can be:
- task-enabling for alignment;
- potentially less adequate for a different source-documentary task;
without contradiction.
